Player guide

Claimora protects your builds. You mark out a rectangle of land with the Claim Wand, and nobody else can break, build or open things there unless you trust them.

Your first claim

Section titled “Your first claim”
  1. Get the wand. New players get it on their first join. Lost it? Type /claim.
  2. Hold it. Claims near you light up with coloured borders, and the action bar shows how many claim blocks you have.
  3. Right-click a block for the first corner.
  4. Look around. The border follows your crosshair, and the action bar shows the size and cost.
  5. Right-click the opposite corner, then right-click once more to confirm.

Left-click goes back a step if you change your mind.

In a hurry? /claim auto claims a square around you in one go (11 × 11 by default).

Claims always cover the whole height of the world, from bedrock to the sky. You only choose the two corners.

Claim blocks

Section titled “Claim blocks”

Land costs claim blocks: one block per square of ground. A 20 × 25 claim costs 500.

  • Everyone starts with 100.
  • You earn 100 more for every hour you actively play (standing AFK doesn’t count).
  • Abandoning a claim gives its blocks back.
  • Subclaims are free, because they’re inside land you already paid for.

See your balance with /claim list, in /claim menu, or on the action bar while holding the wand.

Buying blocks: if your server has an economy, /claim buy (or Buy claim blocks in /claim menu) lets you buy more, for example /claim buy 500. You see the price and confirm first. You see your money, the price and what you’ll have afterwards before you confirm. Bought blocks are yours for good.

(These are the default numbers. Your server may have changed them.)

What a claim protects

Section titled “What a claim protects”

Inside your claim, players you haven’t trusted can’t:

  • break or place blocks, use buckets, or edit signs
  • open chests, barrels, furnaces and other containers
  • use doors, trapdoors, gates, buttons, levers or pressure plates
  • hurt animals, or break item frames, paintings, armor stands or vehicles

Claims also stop explosions, fire spread, and pistons, water or lava pushing in from outside. Crafting tables and ender chests stay usable by everyone, and villager trading is allowed (your server can change both).

If you try to build somewhere you can’t, the claim’s border flashes so you can see whose land it is.

Trusting friends

Section titled “Trusting friends”

Stand in your claim and:

Command What it does
/trust Pick an online player from a list, then pick their level
/trust <player> Trust someone at Build, even if they’re offline
/trust <player> <level> Trust someone at a level: guest, containers, build or manager
/untrust <player> Remove them
/trustlist See who’s trusted and at which level. Tap someone to change their level or remove them.

Levels (each one includes everything above it):

Level They can
Guest Use doors, trapdoors, gates, buttons, levers, pressure plates, beds and bells, and ride vehicles
Containers Guest, plus open chests and other storage, look after animals (feed, breed, shear, milk) and farm: harvest ripe crops and replant them
Build Containers, plus break and place anything
Manager Build, plus trust and untrust other players (up to Build)

Only you, the owner, can resize, rename, change options, give away or abandon the claim. Managers can’t make or remove other Managers.

Trusted players at every level can teleport to and Claim-Fly in your claim, unless the server requires a higher level.

The claim menu

Section titled “The claim menu”

/claim menu opens a menu (a form on Bedrock) with:

  • Your claims: pick one to Teleport or Manage it
  • Trusted claims: claims other players trusted you in
  • Help

Manage lets you change trusted players, subclaims, the name and the home point, give the claim away, or abandon it, all without walking there.

If the server has Claimora’s Bedrock-style menus and you accepted the resource pack, /claim menu looks like Bedrock’s own menus on Java too: a banner with your blocks left, rows for Your claims, Trusted claims, Buy blocks and Help, and a Get claim wand button. Each claim has its own page with Teleport, Set home here, Rename, switches for its options (they save straight away), Trusted players, Banned players, Subclaims, Transfer and Abandon. Trust levels, buying blocks (a slider), bans, renaming and the confirmations use the same style. Rename and “ban an offline player” use Minecraft’s own text box under the page. Long lists (many claims or players) have < Previous and Next > buttons at the bottom.

Bedrock players, and Java players without the pack, see the normal menus with the same options.

The normal menu (without the Bedrock-style menus, or on Bedrock) now also has Get claim wand, and its Buy claim blocks screen has a slider for any amount, with the usual pack buttons below it.

Resizing a claim

Section titled “Resizing a claim”

Hold the wand, right-click a corner of your claim (the border shows where the corners are), move to the new spot, and right-click again. Growing a claim costs the extra blocks. Shrinking it gives blocks back.

Subclaims

Section titled “Subclaims”

A subclaim is a smaller area inside your claim with its own trust list. For example, a storage room only some friends can open.

  • Hold the wand and right-click inside your claim to start one, then pick the corners as usual.
  • Subclaims are private: only you and the players trusted in that subclaim can build there. Players trusted in the main claim can’t, unless you trust them in the subclaim too.
  • Stand inside a subclaim and use /trust to manage its list.
  • Subclaims have yellow borders.

Claim options

Section titled “Claim options”

Each claim has a few settings you can change: /claim options while standing in it, or Manage → Options in /claim menu. Tap a setting to switch it.

Option What it does
Crop trampling Blocked: nobody can trample farmland in the claim, not even you or your trusted players. (Mobs and strangers can never trample your crops anyway.)
Hostile mobs Don’t spawn: zombies, skeletons, creepers and other hostile mobs stop spawning naturally in the claim. Mob spawners still work, and mobs can still walk in from outside.
PvP Off: players can’t hurt each other in the claim, including with arrows and harmful potions.
Claim Fly Blocked: nobody can use /claimfly in the claim, not even you. Handy if you trust friends to build but don’t want them flying around, or for a parkour subclaim.
  • Subclaims start with their main claim’s options and keep following it until you change them. Stand in a subclaim and use /claim options (or Manage → Options on the subclaim) and tap a setting to switch it just there. Setting it back to match the main claim makes it follow the main claim again. For example, block hostile mobs across your base but allow them in a “Mob farm” subclaim, or turn PvP off everywhere except an arena subclaim.
  • Only the owner can change them. Trusted players can’t.
  • Your server may have turned some of these off. They then don’t appear in the menu.

Claim Fly

Section titled “Claim Fly”

If your rank includes it, /claimfly (or /claim fly) lets you fly inside claims you own or are trusted in.

  • Type /claimfly, then double-jump inside your claim to fly. /claimfly again turns it off.
  • Daily time: depending on your rank you get a number of flying minutes per day (or unlimited). Only time spent actually flying counts: standing or walking with Claim Fly on is free. The time resets every day at the same time (midnight Philippine time by default).
  • While you fly, the action bar shows ✈ Claim Fly · 42:17 left. You get a chat warning at 5 and 1 minutes left. When your time runs out mid-air you get the same 3-second countdown and a safe fall.
  • Leaving the claim while flying: you get a 3-second countdown (flight ends in 3…). Fly back in and you keep flying. Otherwise flight stops and you fall, but that fall doesn’t hurt.
  • Walking out: flight pauses and comes back when you walk into an allowed claim again.
  • PvP: hitting another player, or being hit by one, turns Claim Fly off at once (your fall is still safe), and you can’t turn it back on for 10 seconds. Mobs don’t count.
  • /claimfly info shows whether it’s on, your rank tier, whether you can fly where you stand, your time left today and when it resets.
  • Claim owners can block Claim Fly in their claim (see Claim options).

(Your server decides who gets Claim Fly, whether trusted claims count, and the timings.)

Naming and renaming

Section titled “Naming and renaming”

New claims ask for a name (for example “Base”). Skip it and the claim is called “Claim 1”, “Claim 2” and so on. Each of your claims needs a different name.

  • /claim rename opens the name box for the claim you’re standing in.
  • /claim rename <name> renames it straight away.

Teleporting to your claims

Section titled “Teleporting to your claims”
Command What it does
/claim tp Opens the menu so you can pick a claim
/claim tp Base Teleports to your claim called “Base”
/claim tp Base/Storage Teleports to the subclaim “Storage” inside “Base”
/claim sethome Sets where you land in the claim you’re standing in

You stand still for a few seconds while dots fill up in the action bar. Moving or taking damage cancels it. After a teleport there’s a short cooldown.

Without a home point, you land on a safe spot near the middle of the claim. You can also teleport to claims you’re trusted in, if the server allows it.

Some servers turn claim teleport off; then /claim tp says so and the Teleport buttons aren’t in the menus.

Giving a claim away

Section titled “Giving a claim away”

Stand in the claim and type /claim transfer <player>, or use Manage → Transfer in the menu. The new owner must be online and pays the claim blocks. You get yours back. Trusted players and subclaims stay with the claim.

Abandoning a claim

Section titled “Abandoning a claim”

/claim abandon (or Manage → Abandon) deletes the claim or subclaim you’re standing in, after a confirmation. You get its claim blocks back. This can’t be undone.

Stuck in someone’s claim

Section titled “Stuck in someone’s claim”

If you’re trapped inside another player’s claim and can’t build your way out, type /claim trapped. Stand still for 10 seconds (moving or getting hurt cancels it) and you’re moved to the nearest safe spot just outside the claim. You can use it once an hour. If no safe spot is found nearby, nothing happens and you can ask an admin.

Banning players

Section titled “Banning players”

If someone keeps bothering you, ban them from your claim: stand in it and type /claim ban <player>, or open Banned players on the claim’s page in /claim menu.

  • A banned player can’t enter the claim or any of its subclaims, by any route: walking, flying, riding, ender pearls (they get the pearl back) or teleport commands. Walking into it feels like a wall, with a short message and the border shown for a moment. If they’re inside when you ban them, they’re moved just outside.
  • Banning someone also removes their trust in that claim and its subclaims. Unbanning doesn’t bring their trust back; trust them again if you want.
  • Only the claim’s owner can ban. You can’t ban admins.
  • A claim can hold up to 50 bans (the server may change this). Offline players can be banned by name.
  • /claim unban <player> lifts a ban, /claim bans lists them. Tapping a name in the menu also unbans.

Some servers turn bans off; then these commands and the menu row aren’t there.

Border colours

Section titled “Border colours”
Colour Meaning
Green Your claim
Red Someone else’s claim
Yellow A subclaim
Orange A server (admin) claim, such as spawn
Light blue Your selection, and it’s valid
Red selection Your selection overlaps something or is too small

Bedrock players

Section titled “Bedrock players”

Everything works on Bedrock too. Menus and the name box appear as forms. Borders show either as coloured particles or as the same 3D beams Java players see, depending on the server.

FAQ

Section titled “FAQ”

Why can’t I claim here? The usual reasons: you’ve reached your rank’s claim limit, you’re too close to another claim (claims can’t overlap), the area is too small (at least 5 wide and 25 blocks by default), you don’t have enough claim blocks, claiming is turned off in this world, or the land is a protected server region. The action bar tells you which one.

Do I lose my claims if I stop playing? Only if your server turned on inactive-claim expiry. Ask an admin. Expired claims can be restored by an admin for a while afterwards.

I abandoned my base by accident. Abandons can’t be undone, so ask an admin. They can see who owned what, but can’t bring an abandoned claim back.

What are orange claims? Land the server owns, such as spawn or a market. Only players an admin trusted there can build. They don’t belong to any player.

Can I make a claim go up only to a certain height? No. Claims always cover the full height.
